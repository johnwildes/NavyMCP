# Naval Air Station Whidbey Island

## Overview
Naval Air Station Whidbey Island is the premier naval aviation installation in the Pacific Northwest and home to all Navy electronic attack squadrons flying the EA-18G Growler. It is the primary location for electronic warfare training and operations for the U.S. Navy.

## Established
September 21, 1942

## History
Naval Air Station Whidbey Island was established during World War II in 1942 when the Navy needed air bases for defense of the Pacific Northwest. The location was specifically chosen for its strategic position in the Puget Sound area, sheltered waters, and scarcity of fog compared to other coastal areas. During the Cold War, the base became increasingly important as electronic warfare capabilities developed. In the 1970s, Whidbey Island became the home of the new EA-6B Prowler electronic warfare aircraft, solidifying its role as the center for naval electronic warfare. This mission further evolved with the introduction of the EA-18G Growler in the early 2000s, replacing the aging Prowler fleet. Today, NAS Whidbey Island continues to play a critical role in the Navy's electronic warfare capabilities while also supporting maritime patrol operations across the Pacific region.
