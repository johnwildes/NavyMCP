# Naval Base Kitsap

## Overview
Naval Base Kitsap is the third-largest Navy base in the U.S. and the largest naval organization in Navy Region Northwest. It is a strategic submarine base that serves as homeport for both ballistic missile submarines (SSBNs) and fast attack submarines (SSNs).

## Location
- **City/State**: Kitsap County, Washington
- **Region**: Puget Sound
- **Sites**: Bangor, Bremerton, Keyport, Manchester, and Jackson Park
- **Coordinates**: 47°43′58″N 122°41′57″W (Bangor)

- Seawolf-class and Virginia-class fast attack submarines
- USS Nimitz (CVN 68) and other surface ships for maintenance

## Established
2004 (through merging Naval Station Bremerton and Naval Submarine Base Bangor)

## History
Naval Base Kitsap was established in 2004 through the merger of Naval Station Bremerton (est. 1891) and Naval Submarine Base Bangor (est. 1942), creating a consolidated, more efficient installation. However, the naval presence in the area dates back to 1891 when the Navy established the Puget Sound Naval Shipyard in Bremerton. During the Cold War, Bangor became a critical strategic location for the Pacific Fleet's ballistic missile submarine force, serving as a forward base for the nation's nuclear deterrent. The Bangor site was chosen specifically for its deep waters and strategic location to support submarine operations. Today, Naval Base Kitsap continues to be one of the Navy's most important installations, supporting the Pacific Fleet and serving as a critical component of the nation's strategic nuclear deterrent force.
