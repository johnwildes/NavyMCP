# Naval Air Station Oceana

## Overview
Naval Air Station Oceana is a master jet base and the East Coast home for the U.S. Navy's F/A-18 Hornet and F/A-18 Super Hornet fighter jets. It is one of the largest and most advanced air stations in the world, supporting the Atlantic Fleet's strike fighter capability.

## Established
August 17, 1943

## History
Naval Air Station Oceana was originally commissioned in 1943 as a small auxiliary airfield during World War II, supporting operations at Naval Station Norfolk. In the post-war period, the base rapidly expanded as jet aircraft technology developed and the Cold War intensified. By the 1950s, Oceana had become a major East Coast master jet base, home to increasingly sophisticated fighter aircraft. The 1960s and 1970s saw further expansion as the F-14 Tomcat arrived, followed by the F/A-18 Hornet in later decades. Oceana has played a crucial role in naval aviation operations during conflicts including Vietnam, the Gulf War, and the Global War on Terror. Today, NAS Oceana continues its mission as the Navy's East Coast master jet base, despite challenges from encroaching urban development in surrounding Virginia Beach.
