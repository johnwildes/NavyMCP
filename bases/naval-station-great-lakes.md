# Naval Station Great Lakes

## Overview
Naval Station Great Lakes is the largest military installation in Illinois and the home of the United States Navy's only boot camp. Known as "The Quarterdeck of the Navy," it serves as the Navy's premier training center, transforming civilians into sailors through basic and specialized training programs.

## Established
July 1, 1911

## History
Naval Station Great Lakes was established in 1911, largely due to the efforts of local residents and businessman Captain Albert M. Ross, who recognized the need for a naval training station on the Great Lakes. During World War I, the base experienced its first significant expansion as it trained over 125,000 sailors. However, it was during World War II that Great Lakes underwent its most dramatic growth, with the base population swelling to over 100,000 personnel as it trained more than 1 million sailors throughout the conflict. After the war, the base's mission evolved to focus on technical training alongside boot camp. Despite several rounds of military base realignments in recent decades, Naval Station Great Lakes has solidified its position as the Navy's sole recruit training center following the closure of training facilities in Orlando and San Diego in the 1990s. Today, it continues its critical mission of transforming civilians into sailors ready to serve the fleet.
