# Naval Air Station Pensacola

## Overview
Naval Air Station Pensacola, known as the "Cradle of Naval Aviation," is the primary training base for all Navy, Marine Corps, and Coast Guard aviators. Located in Pensacola, Florida, it is the oldest naval air station and home to the famous Blue Angels flight demonstration squadron.

## History
Naval Air Station Pensacola's roots trace back to 1826 when the Pensacola Navy Yard was established. The site was formally commissioned as a naval air station in 1914, becoming the first such installation in the United States. During World War I, the station expanded dramatically, training over 1,000 naval aviators. The interwar period saw further development with the introduction of carrier aviation training. World War II brought explosive growth, with the base training more than 60,000 aviators throughout the conflict. After the war, NAS Pensacola continued as the primary training location for naval aviators, adapting to jet aircraft and helicopter training. Despite threats of closure during various Base Realignment and Closure (BRAC) processes over the decades, the base's historical significance and specialized training capabilities have ensured its continued operation as the cornerstone of naval aviation training.
