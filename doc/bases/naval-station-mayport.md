# Naval Station Mayport

## Overview
Naval Station Mayport is a major United States Navy base located at the mouth of the St. Johns River in Jacksonville, Florida. It is the third-largest naval facility in the continental United States and serves as a crucial homeport for both surface ships and aircraft.

## History
Naval Station Mayport was commissioned in December 1942 during the height of World War II, initially serving as a Naval Sea Frontier base and training center for patrol squadrons. The base was strategically positioned to combat German U-boat threats in the Atlantic. After the war, Mayport was designated as a permanent naval installation in 1948, and development of its harbor and basin began in earnest. The completion of the carrier basin in the 1950s allowed the base to accommodate aircraft carriers, significantly enhancing its strategic importance. Throughout the Cold War and beyond, Naval Station Mayport has evolved into a major operational hub for the Atlantic Fleet, serving as a vital component of America's national defense strategy and a key launching point for naval operations in the Atlantic Ocean, Caribbean Sea, and the Mediterranean.
