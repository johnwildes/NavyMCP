# Naval Base Coronado

## Overview
Naval Base Coronado is a major naval complex in San Diego County, California, consisting of eight installations, with Naval Air Station North Island (NASNI) serving as its headquarters. It is a major aviation and special warfare center of the United States Navy.

## Location
- **City/State**: Coronado and San Diego, California
- **Region**: Southern California
- **Coordinates**: 32°41′N 117°12′W (North Island)

## History
Naval Base Coronado's history is deeply intertwined with naval aviation in the United States. Naval Air Station North Island, its cornerstone facility, was established in 1917 and is often referred to as the "Birthplace of Naval Aviation." The site was chosen by aviation pioneer Glenn Curtiss who set up a flying school there in 1911. During World War II, both North Island and the Naval Amphibious Base expanded dramatically, with the latter becoming the training center for Navy SEALs and other special operations forces. In 1997, eight separate installations were consolidated into Naval Base Coronado as part of the Navy's regional shore establishment reorganization. This consolidation created a more efficient command structure while preserving the unique missions of each installation. Today, Naval Base Coronado continues as one of the Navy's most strategically important bases, supporting both conventional naval forces and elite special operations units.
