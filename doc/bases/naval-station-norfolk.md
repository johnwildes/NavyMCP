# Naval Station Norfolk

## Overview
Naval Station Norfolk is the largest naval base in the world and serves as the home port for numerous ships of the United States Navy's Atlantic Fleet. Located in Norfolk, Virginia, it occupies approximately 3,400 acres of Hampton Roads' peninsula known as Sewell's Point.

## Established
June 28, 1917

## History
Naval Station Norfolk was established during World War I, after Congress appropriated $1.2 million for land purchase in 1917. The base rapidly expanded as the United States entered the war, evolving from marshy terrain to a critical naval installation. During World War II, it experienced unprecedented growth, serving as a key embarkation point for troops and equipment destined for Europe and North Africa. Throughout the Cold War era, the base continued to expand its facilities and strategic importance. Today, Naval Station Norfolk stands as the world's largest naval station, supporting the operational readiness of the U.S. Atlantic Fleet and serving as a crucial hub for naval activities around the globe.
