# Naval Base San Diego

## Overview
Naval Base San Diego, known as the "Fleet's Home Port," is the second-largest surface ship base of the United States Navy and the principal homeport of the Pacific Fleet. The base is located in San Diego, California along San Diego Bay.

## Established
1922 (as U.S. Destroyer Base)

## History
Naval Base San Diego began as a modest facility in 1922, originally commissioned as a repair and maintenance facility for destroyers. After the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor in 1941, the base underwent massive expansion as part of the war effort, transforming into a major logistics hub for the Pacific theater of World War II. By the end of the war, the base had grown significantly in both size and strategic importance. Throughout the Cold War period and subsequent conflicts, Naval Base San Diego continued to develop into what is now the principal homeport of the Pacific Fleet, supporting over 50 ships and more than 150 tenant commands. The base has played a pivotal role in projecting American naval power across the Pacific Ocean and beyond for nearly a century.
