# Naval Air Station Jacksonville

## Overview
Naval Air Station Jacksonville (NAS Jax) is a major United States Navy base located in Jacksonville, Florida. It is one of the Navy's key installations for maritime patrol and reconnaissance aircraft, and houses numerous aviation and support units.

## History
Naval Air Station Jacksonville was commissioned in October 1940, shortly before America's entry into World War II. The base was built on the site of a defunct Florida National Guard camp along the St. Johns River. During World War II, NAS Jacksonville rapidly expanded, becoming a vital training center for naval aviators and a hub for anti-submarine warfare operations in the Atlantic. At its peak during the war, the station trained more than 20,000 pilots and aircrewmen annually. Throughout the Cold War, the base continued to evolve, transitioning from propeller aircraft to jets and eventually becoming home to the Navy's maritime patrol aircraft community. Despite significant restructuring of military bases nationwide in recent decades, NAS Jacksonville has not only survived but thrived, solidifying its position as a cornerstone of naval aviation operations on the East Coast.
