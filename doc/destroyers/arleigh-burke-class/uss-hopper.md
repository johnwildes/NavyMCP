# USS Hopper (DDG-70)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight I)
**Namesake:** Rear Admiral Grace Hopper
**Commissioned:** September 6, 1997
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Pearl Harbor, Hawaii

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 8,230 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 505 ft (154 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/54 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 1 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 1 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopter

## History
USS Hopper is the twentieth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Rear Admiral Grace Hopper, a pioneering computer scientist and naval officer known as "Amazing Grace" and the "mother of computing." Grace Hopper was a key figure in developing the first computer programming language compiler and the COBOL programming language.

The ship was commissioned on September 6, 1997, and is homeported at Naval Station Pearl Harbor, Hawaii. USS Hopper has participated in numerous operations including deployments to the Western Pacific and Middle East, and is equipped with advanced Aegis combat systems for ballistic missile defense.
