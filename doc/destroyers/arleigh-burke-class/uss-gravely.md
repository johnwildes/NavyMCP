# USS Gravely (DDG-107)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Vice Admiral Samuel L. Gravely Jr.
**Commissioned:** November 20, 2010
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Gravely is the fifty-seventh ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Vice Admiral Samuel L. Gravely Jr., who was the first African American to command a U.S. Navy warship, the first African American to command a U.S. fleet, and the first African American to become a flag officer in the U.S. Navy.

The ship was commissioned on November 20, 2010, and has participated in numerous operations including deployments to the Mediterranean Sea and the Middle East. USS Gravely has served as the flagship for Standing NATO Maritime Group 1 and has conducted maritime security operations, ballistic missile defense, and multinational exercises with allied navies.
