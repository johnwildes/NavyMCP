# USS Chung-Hoon (DDG-93)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Rear Admiral Gordon Pai'ea Chung-Hoon
**Commissioned:** September 18, 2004
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Chung-Hoon is the forty-third ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Rear Admiral Gordon Pai'ea Chung-Hoon, a Naval Academy graduate who received the Navy Cross and Silver Star for his actions during World War II. He was the first Asian American flag officer in the U.S. Navy and a distinguished native of Hawaii.

The ship was commissioned on September 18, 2004, and was formerly homeported at Naval Station Pearl Harbor, Hawaii before being reassigned to San Diego. USS Chung-Hoon has participated in numerous operations throughout the Pacific and has been involved in counter-piracy missions and freedom of navigation operations.
