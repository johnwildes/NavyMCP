# USS John S. McCain (DDG-56)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight I)
**Namesake:** John S. McCain Sr., John S. McCain Jr., and John S. McCain III
**Commissioned:** July 2, 1994
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Everett, Washington

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 8,230 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 505 ft (154 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/54 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 1 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 1 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopter

## History
USS John S. McCain is the sixth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for three generations of John S. McCain: Admiral John S. McCain Sr. and Admiral John S. McCain Jr., both distinguished naval officers, and U.S. Senator John S. McCain III, who served as a naval aviator during the Vietnam War. The ship was commissioned on July 2, 1994.
