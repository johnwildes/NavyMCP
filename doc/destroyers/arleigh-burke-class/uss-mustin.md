# USS Mustin (DDG-89)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** The Mustin family (naval dynasty)
**Commissioned:** July 26, 2003
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Mustin is the thirty-ninth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for the Mustin family, a naval dynasty that includes Captain Henry C. Mustin, Vice Admiral Lloyd M. Mustin, and Lieutenant Commander Thomas M. Mustin, who collectively served from the early 20th century through the Vietnam War era.

The ship was commissioned on July 26, 2003, and was formerly forward-deployed to Yokosuka, Japan as part of the U.S. 7th Fleet before being reassigned to San Diego. USS Mustin has participated in numerous operations throughout the Western Pacific and has conducted freedom of navigation operations in the South China Sea and Taiwan Strait. The ship is equipped with advanced Aegis combat systems and ballistic missile defense capabilities.
