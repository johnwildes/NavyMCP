# USS Shoup (DDG-86)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** General David M. Shoup
**Commissioned:** June 22, 2002
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Shoup is the thirty-sixth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for General David M. Shoup, who was awarded the Medal of Honor for his actions as commanding officer of the 2nd Marines at the Battle of Tarawa during World War II. General Shoup later served as the 22nd Commandant of the Marine Corps from 1960 to 1963.

The ship was commissioned on June 22, 2002, and has participated in numerous operations including deployments to the Western Pacific and Middle East. USS Shoup has been involved in various multinational exercises and humanitarian missions throughout her service.
