# USS Preble (DDG-88)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Commodore Edward Preble
**Commissioned:** November 9, 2002
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Preble is the thirty-eighth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Commodore Edward Preble, who served with distinction during the Barbary Wars and the War of 1812. His leadership during the First Barbary War established the U.S. Navy's reputation for professionalism and fighting spirit.

The ship was commissioned on November 9, 2002, and was formerly homeported at Naval Station Pearl Harbor, Hawaii before being reassigned to San Diego. USS Preble has participated in numerous operations throughout the Pacific and has been equipped with advanced ballistic missile defense capabilities. In recent years, she has conducted freedom of navigation operations in the Western Pacific and participated in multinational exercises with allied navies.
