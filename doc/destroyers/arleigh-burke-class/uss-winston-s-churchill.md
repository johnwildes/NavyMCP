# USS Winston S. Churchill (DDG-81)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Sir Winston Churchill
**Commissioned:** March 10, 2001
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Winston S. Churchill is the thirty-first ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers and the third Flight IIA variant. She is named after the British Prime Minister Sir Winston Churchill and is one of only a few U.S. Navy ships named after a foreign national.

The ship was commissioned on March 10, 2001, and carries a unique distinction of having a Royal Navy officer permanently assigned to the ship, a tradition that dates back to her commissioning. USS Winston S. Churchill also has both the U.S. and British flags flying on board and carries a ship's bell from the former HMS Churchill.
