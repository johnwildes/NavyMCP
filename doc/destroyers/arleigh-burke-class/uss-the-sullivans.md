# USS The Sullivans (DDG-68)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight I)
**Namesake:** The five Sullivan brothers
**Commissioned:** April 19, 1997
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Mayport, Florida

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 8,230 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 505 ft (154 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/54 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 1 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 1 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopter

## History
USS The Sullivans is the eighteenth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for the five Sullivan brothers (George, Francis, Joseph, Madison, and Albert) who were killed in action when their ship, USS Juneau, was sunk by a Japanese submarine during the Naval Battle of Guadalcanal on November 13, 1942. This is the second ship named for the Sullivan brothers.

The ship was commissioned on April 19, 1997, and has participated in numerous operations including deployments to the Mediterranean Sea, the Persian Gulf, and counter-narcotics operations in the Caribbean.
