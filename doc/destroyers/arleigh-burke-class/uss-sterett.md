# USS Sterett (DDG-104)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Lieutenant Andrew Sterett
**Commissioned:** August 9, 2008
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Sterett is the fifty-fourth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is the fourth ship named for Lieutenant Andrew Sterett (1778–1807), who served with distinction during the Quasi-War with France and the First Barbary War. Sterett commanded the USS Enterprise in a notable victory over the Tripolitan vessel Tripoli in 1801.

The ship was commissioned on August 9, 2008, and has participated in numerous operations throughout the Pacific and Middle East. USS Sterett has served as part of carrier strike groups and has conducted anti-piracy operations in the Gulf of Aden. The ship is equipped with advanced Aegis combat systems and ballistic missile defense capabilities.
