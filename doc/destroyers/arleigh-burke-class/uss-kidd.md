# USS Kidd (DDG-100)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Rear Admiral Isaac C. Kidd
**Commissioned:** June 9, 2007
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base Everett, Washington

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Kidd is the fiftieth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Rear Admiral Isaac C. Kidd, who was killed on the bridge of USS Arizona during the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941. Admiral Kidd was posthumously awarded the Medal of Honor for his actions during the attack.

The ship was commissioned on June 9, 2007, and has participated in numerous operations throughout the Pacific. In 2020, USS Kidd gained attention when she experienced a COVID-19 outbreak during the global pandemic, becoming one of the first U.S. Navy ships to deal with the virus while deployed. The ship is equipped with advanced Aegis combat systems and ballistic missile defense capabilities.
