# USS McFaul (DDG-74)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight I)
**Namesake:** Chief Petty Officer Donald L. McFaul
**Commissioned:** April 25, 1998
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 8,230 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 505 ft (154 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/54 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 1 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 1 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopter

## History
USS McFaul is the twenty-fourth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Chief Petty Officer Donald L. McFaul, a Navy SEAL who was posthumously awarded the Navy Cross for heroism during Operation Just Cause in Panama in 1989. When his unit came under fire, McFaul sacrificed his life to save a wounded teammate.

The ship was commissioned on April 25, 1998, and has participated in numerous operations including counter-narcotic operations in the Caribbean and humanitarian assistance following the 2010 Haiti earthquake.
