# USS Enterprise (CVN-80)

## Characteristics
- **Ship Name**: USS Enterprise
- **Hull Number**: CVN-80
- **Commissioned**: Expected 2028
- **Displacement**: Approximately 100,000 long tons (100,000 t) full load
- **Length**: 1,106 feet (337 m)
- **Beam**: 134 feet (41 m) waterline, 256 feet (78 m) extreme
- **Draft**: 39 feet (12 m)
- **Power Plant**: Two A1B nuclear reactors, four shafts
- **Speed**: 30+ knots (56+ km/h)
- **Crew Size**: 2,600 (ship only; approximately 4,400 with full air wing)
- **Aircraft**: 75+ aircraft including F-35C Lightning II, F/A-18E/F Super Hornets, EA-18G Growlers, E-2D Advanced Hawkeyes, and helicopters

## History
The USS Enterprise (CVN-80) is the third ship of the Ford-class and the ninth U.S. Navy vessel to bear this historic name. Construction began in 2022, with steel cutting ceremonies marking the start of manufacturing. The Enterprise carries forward the legacy of the previous USS Enterprise (CVN-65), which was the world's first nuclear-powered aircraft carrier and served for over 50 years. This new carrier will benefit significantly from design and construction improvements identified during the building of her sister ships USS Gerald R. Ford and USS John F. Kennedy. The Navy expects construction time and costs to be reduced through economies of scale, modular construction techniques, and digital design advancements. When completed, the Enterprise will incorporate all the technological advancements of the Ford-class, including the Electromagnetic Aircraft Launch System, Advanced Arresting Gear, and increased automation, while continuing the proud tradition of one of the most storied names in U.S. naval history.
