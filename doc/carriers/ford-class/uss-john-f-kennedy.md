# USS John F. Kennedy (CVN-79)

## Characteristics
- **Ship Name**: USS John F. Kennedy
- **Hull Number**: CVN-79
- **Commissioned**: Expected 2025
- **Displacement**: Approximately 100,000 long tons (100,000 t) full load
- **Length**: 1,106 feet (337 m)
- **Beam**: 134 feet (41 m) waterline, 256 feet (78 m) extreme
- **Draft**: 39 feet (12 m)
- **Power Plant**: Two A1B nuclear reactors, four shafts
- **Speed**: 30+ knots (56+ km/h)
- **Crew Size**: 2,600 (ship only; approximately 4,400 with full air wing)
- **Aircraft**: 75+ aircraft including F-35C Lightning II, F/A-18E/F Super Hornets, EA-18G Growlers, E-2D Advanced Hawkeyes, and helicopters

## History
The USS John F. Kennedy (CVN-79) is the second ship of the Ford-class and the second aircraft carrier named after the 35th President of the United States. Construction began in 2011, with the ship christened on December 7, 2019. Unlike its predecessor, the Kennedy is being built using digital technologies and incorporating lessons learned from the Ford's construction, which has helped reduce costs and construction time. The carrier features the same advanced technologies as the USS Gerald R. Ford, including the Electromagnetic Aircraft Launch System, Advanced Arresting Gear, and increased automation allowing for a smaller crew. As of early 2025, the Kennedy is in the final stages of outfitting and testing as it prepares for commissioning into the fleet. Upon completion, the Kennedy will serve for an expected 50-year service life, advancing the Navy's goal of maintaining a 12-carrier fleet and ensuring continued American naval superiority through the mid-21st century.
