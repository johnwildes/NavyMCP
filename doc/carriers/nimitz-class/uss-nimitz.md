# USS Nimitz (CVN-68)

## Characteristics
- **Ship Name**: USS Nimitz
- **Hull Number**: CVN-68
- **Commissioned**: May 3, 1975
- **Displacement**: Approximately 100,000 long tons (100,000 t) full load
- **Length**: 1,092 feet (332.8 m)
- **Beam**: 134 feet (40.8 m) waterline, 252 feet (76.8 m) extreme
- **Draft**: 37 feet (11.3 m)
- **Power Plant**: 2 A4W nuclear reactors, 4 steam turbines, 4 shafts, 260,000 shaft horsepower
- **Speed**: 30+ knots (56+ km/h)
- **Crew Size**: Approximately 5,000 (Ship's company: 3,200, Air wing: 1,800)
- **Aircraft**: 60+ aircraft including F/A-18 Super Hornets, EA-18G Growlers, E-2 Hawkeyes, and helicopters

## History
The USS Nimitz (CVN-68) is the lead ship of the Nimitz-class aircraft carriers and is one of the largest warships in the world. Named after Fleet Admiral Chester W. Nimitz, she was commissioned on May 3, 1975. The Nimitz has been involved in numerous military operations including the Iran hostage crisis where she participated in Operation Eagle Claw in 1980, served in the Persian Gulf during the Iran-Iraq war in the 1980s, and played a significant role in Operations Desert Shield and Desert Storm in the early 1990s. The carrier also supported Operation Iraqi Freedom and deployed to the Western Pacific numerous times. Throughout her service, USS Nimitz has been a cornerstone of American naval power projection and diplomatic influence around the globe.
