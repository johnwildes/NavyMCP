# USS Abraham Lincoln (CVN-72)

## Characteristics
- **Ship Name**: USS Abraham Lincoln
- **Hull Number**: CVN-72
- **Commissioned**: November 11, 1989
- **Displacement**: Approximately 100,000 long tons (100,000 t) full load
- **Length**: 1,092 feet (332.8 m)
- **Beam**: 134 feet (40.8 m) waterline, 252 feet (76.8 m) extreme
- **Draft**: 37 feet (11.3 m)
- **Power Plant**: 2 A4W nuclear reactors, 4 steam turbines, 4 shafts, 260,000 shaft horsepower
- **Speed**: 30+ knots (56+ km/h)
- **Crew Size**: Approximately 5,000 (Ship's company: 3,200, Air wing: 1,800)
- **Aircraft**: 60+ aircraft including F/A-18 Super Hornets, EA-18G Growlers, E-2 Hawkeyes, and helicopters

## History
The USS Abraham Lincoln (CVN-72) was commissioned on November 11, 1989, named after the 16th President of the United States. The carrier achieved prominence during the Iraq War when President George W. Bush delivered his "Mission Accomplished" speech on its deck on May 1, 2003. The Lincoln has been deployed extensively to the Persian Gulf and Western Pacific, participating in Operations Desert Storm, Southern Watch, and Enduring Freedom. In 2011, it was deployed as part of relief efforts following the Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami in Japan. The carrier underwent a four-year Refueling and Complex Overhaul (RCOH) from 2013 to 2017, extending its service life and modernizing its capabilities for continued operations into the mid-21st century. After the refit, it was redeployed to the Pacific Fleet and has continued to serve as a cornerstone of American naval power.
