# Newest SSGN Submarines in the US Navy

The United States Navy operates some of the most advanced guided-missile submarines (SSGNs) in the world. These submarines are equipped with state-of-the-art technology and are capable of performing a wide range of missions, including precision strike, intelligence gathering, and special operations support.

## Ohio-Class SSGNs

The Ohio-class submarines were originally designed as ballistic missile submarines (SSBNs) but were later converted into guided-missile submarines (SSGNs). These submarines are among the newest and most capable SSGNs in the US Navy.

### Key Features
- **Missile Capacity**: Each Ohio-class SSGN can carry up to 154 Tomahawk cruise missiles.
- **Special Operations Support**: Equipped with lockout chambers for deploying Navy SEALs and other special operations forces.
- **Advanced Sensors**: State-of-the-art sonar and electronic warfare systems.

These submarines represent a critical component of the US Navy's undersea warfare capabilities and play a vital role in maintaining global security.
