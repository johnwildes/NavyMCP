# CMV-22B Osprey

**Type:** Tiltrotor carrier onboard delivery (COD) aircraft
**Manufacturer:** Bell Boeing
**First Flight:** March 19, 2018 (CMV-22B variant)
**Introduction to Service:** 2020
**Status:** Active

## Variants
- **CMV-22B**: U.S. Navy carrier onboard delivery (COD) variant of the V-22 Osprey

## Specifications
- **Crew:** 3-4 (pilot, copilot, crew chief/loadmaster, flight engineer)
- **Passenger Capacity:** 22 passengers
- **Cargo Capacity:** Up to 10,000 lb (4,536 kg) internally or 15,000 lb (6,800 kg) externally
- **Length:** 57 ft 4 in (17.5 m)
- **Width:** 84 ft 7 in (25.8 m) with rotors turning
- **Height:** 22 ft 1 in (6.7 m)
- **Empty Weight:** 35,000 lb (15,876 kg)
- **Max Takeoff Weight:** 60,500 lb (27,443 kg)
- **Powerplant:** 2 × Rolls-Royce Liberty AE1107C turboshaft engines, 6,150 shp (4,590 kW) each

## Performance
- **Maximum Speed:** 280 knots (322 mph, 519 km/h)
- **Cruise Speed:** 240 knots (276 mph, 445 km/h)
- **Range:** 1,150 nmi (1,323 mi, 2,130 km)
- **Combat Radius:** 530 nmi (610 mi, 982 km)
- **Service Ceiling:** 25,000 ft (7,620 m)
- **Rate of Climb:** 2,320 ft/min (11.8 m/s)

## Fleet Information
- **Inventory:** Approximately 48 planned, 38 in active service (as of 2025)
- **Unit Cost:** $90 million (2023)

## Deployment
The CMV-22B Osprey is deployed aboard aircraft carriers to transport personnel, mail, supplies, and high-priority cargo, particularly aircraft engines, between carriers and shore bases.

### Primary Deployment Locations:
- **Naval Air Station North Island, California**
  - VRM-50 "Sunhawks" (Fleet Replacement Squadron)
  - VRM-30 "Titans"
- **Naval Air Station Norfolk, Virginia**
  - VRM-40 "Mighty Bison"

## Mission
The CMV-22B Osprey serves as the U.S. Navy's carrier onboard delivery (COD) aircraft, replacing the C-2A Greyhound. Its missions include:

- Carrier onboard delivery (COD) of personnel and cargo
- Transport of F-35C power modules and other oversized cargo
- Long-range logistics support for carrier strike groups
- Medical evacuation (MEDEVAC)
- Personnel transport to and from deployed vessels
- Special mission support

## Features
- **Tiltrotor Technology:** Vertical takeoff and landing combined with fixed-wing cruising efficiency
- **Extended Range:** Additional fuel tanks compared to standard V-22 variants
- **High-Speed Ramp:** For rapid loading and unloading of cargo
- **Cargo Handling System:** Rollers and tie-downs for securing cargo
- **Beyond Line-of-Sight Communications:** Enhanced long-range communication systems
- **Public Address System:** For passenger briefings
- **Cabin Pressurization:** For high-altitude flight with passengers
- **Defensive Systems:** Advanced electronic countermeasures
- **Night Vision System:** For night operations
- **Weather Radar:** For safe operation in adverse conditions

## History
The CMV-22B is the Navy variant of the V-22 Osprey, specifically designed for the carrier onboard delivery (COD) mission. Development began in 2015 after the Navy selected the V-22 as the replacement for the aging C-2A Greyhound fleet. The first CMV-22B was delivered to the Navy in 2020, and achieved initial operational capability in 2021. The CMV-22B's unique tiltrotor capability allows it to operate from any flight deck that can accommodate helicopters, expanding delivery options beyond just aircraft carriers to include amphibious assault ships, logistics vessels, and shore bases without conventional runways. The aircraft's ability to carry the F-35C's engine power module—which doesn't fit in the C-2A Greyhound—was a significant factor in its selection. By 2025, the CMV-22B has become the primary carrier onboard delivery platform for the Navy, with ongoing deployments supporting carrier strike groups worldwide.
