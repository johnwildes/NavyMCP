# P-8A Poseidon

**Type:** Maritime patrol and reconnaissance aircraft
**Manufacturer:** Boeing Defense, Space & Security
**First Flight:** April 25, 2009
**Introduction to Service:** 2013
**Status:** Active

## Variants
- **P-8A**: Standard U.S. Navy variant
- **P-8I Neptune**: Indian Navy variant

## Specifications
- **Crew:** 9 (5 officers, 4 enlisted)
- **Length:** 129 ft 5 in (39.47 m)
- **Wingspan:** 123 ft 6 in (37.64 m)
- **Height:** 42 ft 1 in (12.83 m)
- **Empty Weight:** 138,300 lb (62,730 kg)
- **Max Takeoff Weight:** 189,200 lb (85,820 kg)
- **Powerplant:** 2 × CFM56-7B27A turbofan engines, 27,000 lbf (120 kN) thrust each

## Performance
- **Maximum Speed:** 490 knots (564 mph, 907 km/h)
- **Cruise Speed:** 440 knots (510 mph, 815 km/h)
- **Range:** 1,200 nmi (1,381 mi, 2,222 km) with 4 hours on station
- **Ferry Range:** 4,500 nmi (5,182 mi, 8,334 km)
- **Service Ceiling:** 41,000 ft (12,500 m)

## Armament
- **Internal weapons bay:** 5 stations for torpedoes, depth charges, or mines
- **External hardpoints:** 6 wing stations for missiles, torpedoes, bombs, or mines
- **Weapons capacity:** Up to 22,000 lb (9,979 kg)

**Typical loadouts include:**
- **Torpedoes:** Mk 54 lightweight torpedoes
- **Anti-ship missiles:** AGM-84 Harpoon
- **Land-attack missiles:** AGM-158C LRASM
- **Bombs:** Mk 82 depth bombs
- **Mines:** Quickstrike naval mines

## Fleet Information
- **Inventory:** Approximately 130 in active service (as of 2025)
- **Unit Cost:** $175 million (2023)

## Mission
The P-8A Poseidon serves as the U.S. Navy's primary aircraft for:

- Anti-submarine warfare (ASW)
- Anti-surface warfare (ASuW)
- Maritime intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR)
- Search and rescue (SAR)
- Communications relay
- Command and control functions
- Humanitarian assistance and disaster relief support

## Features
- **Advanced Radar:** AN/APY-10 multi-mode radar for high-resolution imaging, surveillance, and tracking
- **Acoustic Systems:** Advanced acoustic processor and sonobuoy launching system
- **Electro-Optical/Infrared (EO/IR):** MX-20HD digital multi-spectral sensor system
- **Electronic Support Measures (ESM):** ALQ-240 Electronic Support Measures suite
- **Communications:** Extensive secure communications capabilities including Link-16
- **Automated Identification System (AIS):** For tracking maritime traffic
- **Mission Systems:** Advanced mission computing systems and integrated tactical picture
- **Weapons Systems:** Integrated stores management system for weapons control
- **Air-to-Air Refueling:** Capable of extended missions with refueling

## History
The P-8A Poseidon was developed as the replacement for the aging P-3C Orion maritime patrol aircraft. Based on the Boeing 737-800 commercial airframe, the P-8A represents a significant technological advancement in maritime patrol and reconnaissance capabilities. The first aircraft was delivered to the U.S. Navy in 2012 and achieved initial operational capability in 2013. The transition from P-3C to P-8A was completed in 2023, marking a full modernization of the Navy's maritime patrol and reconnaissance force. The aircraft has seen operational deployments worldwide, providing enhanced capabilities in anti-submarine warfare, intelligence gathering, and maritime domain awareness. The P-8A is expected to remain in service until at least the 2050s, with ongoing updates to its mission systems and capabilities.
