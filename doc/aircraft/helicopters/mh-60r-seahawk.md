# MH-60R Seahawk

**Type:** Multi-mission maritime helicopter
**Manufacturer:** Sikorsky Aircraft (Lockheed Martin)
**First Flight:** 2001
**Introduction to Service:** 2006
**Status:** Active

## Variants
- **MH-60R**: Primary maritime helicopter for the U.S. Navy

## Specifications
- **Crew:** 3-4 (2 pilots, 1-2 sensor operators)
- **Length:** 64 ft 10 in (19.76 m)
- **Height:** 17 ft 2 in (5.23 m)
- **Main Rotor Diameter:** 53 ft 8 in (16.36 m)
- **Empty Weight:** 15,200 lb (6,895 kg)
- **Max Takeoff Weight:** 23,500 lb (10,659 kg)
- **Powerplant:** 2 × General Electric T700-GE-401C turboshaft engines, 1,800 shp (1,340 kW) each

## Performance
- **Maximum Speed:** 180 knots (207 mph, 333 km/h)
- **Cruise Speed:** 168 knots (193 mph, 311 km/h)
- **Range:** 450 nmi (518 mi, 834 km)
- **Service Ceiling:** 13,000 ft (3,962 m)
- **Rate of Climb:** 1,650 ft/min (8.38 m/s)

## Armament
- **Torpedoes:** Mk 46, Mk 50, or Mk 54 lightweight torpedoes
- **Missiles:** AGM-114 Hellfire anti-surface missiles
- **Other:** M240D 7.62mm machine gun or GAU-21 .50 caliber machine gun

## Fleet Information
- **Inventory:** Approximately 280 in active service (as of 2025)
- **Unit Cost:** $44 million (2023)

## Mission
The MH-60R Seahawk is the U.S. Navy's primary anti-submarine warfare and anti-surface warfare helicopter. Primary missions include:

- Anti-submarine warfare (ASW)
- Anti-surface warfare (ASuW)
- Naval special warfare (NSW) support
- Search and rescue (SAR)
- Communications relay
- Logistics support
- Personnel transport
- Medical evacuation (MEDEVAC)

## Features
- **Multi-Mode Radar:** AN/APS-153 with inverse synthetic aperture radar (ISAR) and periscope detection modes
- **Sonar Systems:** Airborne low-frequency sonar (ALFS) and sonobuoy processing systems
- **Electronic Support Measures:** Advanced radar warning receivers and electronic countermeasures
- **Electro-Optical/Infrared (EO/IR):** Forward-looking infrared (FLIR) and laser designator
- **Communications:** Link-16 tactical data network capability
- **Glass Cockpit:** Advanced digital flight controls and multi-function displays

## History
The MH-60R Seahawk, or "Romeo," is the result of upgrading and improving the SH-60B Seahawk platform. It entered service in 2006 as part of the U.S. Navy's "Helicopter Master Plan," which aimed to reduce the types of helicopters operated by the Navy while increasing capability. The MH-60R represents a significant advancement in naval helicopter technology, combining the missions that were previously performed by multiple helicopter types. Today, it is the backbone of the Navy's airborne anti-submarine and anti-surface warfare capabilities.
