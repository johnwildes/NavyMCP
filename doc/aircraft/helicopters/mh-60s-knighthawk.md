# MH-60S Knighthawk

**Type:** Multi-mission utility helicopter
**Manufacturer:** Sikorsky Aircraft (Lockheed Martin)
**First Flight:** 1997
**Introduction to Service:** 2002
**Status:** Active

## Variants
- **MH-60S**: Naval utility helicopter for the U.S. Navy

## Specifications
- **Crew:** 4 (2 pilots, 2 aircrew)
- **Length:** 64 ft 10 in (19.76 m)
- **Height:** 17 ft 2 in (5.23 m)
- **Main Rotor Diameter:** 53 ft 8 in (16.36 m)
- **Empty Weight:** 14,070 lb (6,382 kg)
- **Max Takeoff Weight:** 23,500 lb (10,659 kg)
- **Powerplant:** 2 × General Electric T700-GE-401C turboshaft engines, 1,800 shp (1,340 kW) each

## Performance
- **Maximum Speed:** 180 knots (207 mph, 333 km/h)
- **Cruise Speed:** 150 knots (173 mph, 278 km/h)
- **Range:** 450 nmi (518 mi, 834 km)
- **Service Ceiling:** 12,000 ft (3,658 m)
- **Rate of Climb:** 1,450 ft/min (7.4 m/s)

## Armament
- **Machine Guns:** M240D 7.62mm or GAU-21 .50 caliber machine guns
- **Missiles:** AGM-114 Hellfire missiles
- **Rockets:** 70 mm Hydra rockets
- **Other:** Precision Strike Package for armed escort missions

- **Unit Cost:** $28 million (2023)

## Mission
The MH-60S Knighthawk, also known as the "Sierra," serves as the U.S. Navy's primary utility helicopter. Primary missions include:

- Vertical replenishment (VERTREP)
- Combat search and rescue (CSAR)
- Naval special warfare (NSW) support
- Airborne mine countermeasures (AMCM)
- Anti-surface warfare (ASuW)
- Humanitarian assistance
- Disaster relief
- Medical evacuation (MEDEVAC)
- Personnel transport

## Features
- **Airborne Mine Countermeasures (AMCM) Systems:** Multiple mine detection and neutralization systems
- **Electro-Optical/Infrared (EO/IR) Systems:** Forward-looking infrared (FLIR) and laser designator
- **Cargo Hook:** External lift capability of up to 9,000 pounds
- **Cabin Configuration:** Flexible arrangements for cargo or personnel transport (up to 12 passengers)
- **Glass Cockpit:** Advanced digital flight controls and multi-function displays
- **Deployable Systems:** Common Data Link (CDL) for real-time intelligence sharing
- **Survivability Equipment:** Radar warning receivers, chaff/flare dispensers, and infrared countermeasures

## History
The MH-60S Knighthawk was developed as part of the Navy's Helicopter Master Plan to replace multiple helicopter types, including the CH-46 Sea Knight. It first entered service in 2002, combining a UH-60L Black Hawk airframe with the dynamics system of the SH-60B Seahawk. Initially focused on vertical replenishment missions, the MH-60S has evolved into a multi-mission platform capable of both logistics support and combat operations. The helicopter has been continuously upgraded with new systems and capabilities, particularly for mine countermeasures and combat support roles, making it one of the most versatile aircraft in the Navy's inventory.
