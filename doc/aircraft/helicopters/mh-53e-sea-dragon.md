# MH-53E Sea Dragon

**Type:** Heavy-lift mine countermeasures helicopter
**Manufacturer:** Sikorsky Aircraft
**First Flight:** 1974 (modified RH-53D)
**Introduction to Service:** 1986
**Status:** Active

## Variants
- **MH-53E**: Mine countermeasures and heavy-lift variant for the U.S. Navy

## Specifications
- **Crew:** 2 pilots, 1-2 aircrewmen
- **Length:** 99 ft 1 in (30.2 m)
- **Height:** 28 ft 4 in (8.64 m)
- **Main Rotor Diameter:** 79 ft (24 m)
- **Empty Weight:** 33,226 lb (15,070 kg)
- **Max Takeoff Weight:** 73,500 lb (33,339 kg)
- **Powerplant:** 3 × General Electric T64-GE-416/416A turboshaft engines, 4,380 shp (3,270 kW) each

## Performance
- **Maximum Speed:** 170 knots (196 mph, 315 km/h)
- **Cruise Speed:** 150 knots (173 mph, 278 km/h)
- **Range:** 540 nmi (621 mi, 1,000 km)
- **Service Ceiling:** 10,000 ft (3,048 m)
- **Rate of Climb:** 1,700 ft/min (8.6 m/s)

## Armament
- **Defensive:** GAU-21 .50 caliber machine gun (door mounted)
- **Mine Countermeasures Systems:** Various towed mine-hunting and mine-sweeping systems

## Fleet Information
- **Inventory:** Approximately 20 in active service (as of 2025)
- **Unit Cost:** $52 million (replacement cost)

## Deployment
The MH-53E Sea Dragon is primarily deployed for airborne mine countermeasures missions and heavy vertical replenishment operations.

### Primary Deployment Locations:
- **Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia**
  - HM-12 "Sea Dragons"
  - HM-14 "Vanguard"
- **Forward deployed units rotate to:**
  - Naval Air Facility Atsugi, Japan
  - Naval Support Activity Bahrain

## Mission
The MH-53E Sea Dragon is the U.S. Navy's primary airborne mine countermeasures platform. Primary missions include:

- Airborne mine countermeasures (AMCM)
- Heavy vertical replenishment (Heavy VERTREP)
- Ship-to-shore transport of equipment and personnel
- Assault support
- Humanitarian assistance and disaster relief

## Features
- **Mine Countermeasures Equipment:** Various towed sleds and systems for detecting and neutralizing sea mines
- **Cargo Capacity:** External hook load up to 27,000 pounds (12,247 kg)
- **Cabin Space:** Large cabin area capable of carrying cargo or up to 55 passengers
- **Aerial Refueling:** In-flight refueling capability
- **Communications:** Secure voice and data systems
- **Navigation Systems:** GPS, inertial navigation system, and Doppler radar
- **Survivability Equipment:** Radar warning receivers, infrared countermeasures, and ballistic protection

## History
The MH-53E Sea Dragon was developed from the CH-53E Super Stallion to meet the U.S. Navy's need for a heavy-lift mine countermeasures helicopter. First deployed in 1986, it replaced the RH-53D Sea Stallion for mine-sweeping operations. The Sea Dragon features enlarged sponsons for increased fuel capacity, improved flight controls, and specialized equipment for detecting and neutralizing sea mines. Despite plans for replacement by the MCM mission package of the Littoral Combat Ship program, the Sea Dragon continues to serve as the Navy's primary airborne mine countermeasures platform. The fleet is undergoing service life extension programs to maintain operational capability until suitable replacements are fully fielded.
