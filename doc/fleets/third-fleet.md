# U.S. 3rd Fleet

## Overview
The United States Third Fleet is a numbered fleet in the United States Navy that controls naval forces in the Eastern and Northern Pacific Ocean. It is one of the largest and most operationally active numbered fleets, providing the realistic, relevant training necessary for an effective global Navy.

## Area of Operations
- **Primary Region**: Eastern and Northern Pacific Ocean
- **Coverage Area**: Approximately 50 million square miles of Pacific Ocean from the U.S. West Coast to the International Date Line
- **Strategic Focus**: Maritime security operations, disaster relief, and theater security cooperation activities with partners in the Pacific

## Personnel
- **Active Duty**: ~800-1,000 personnel (headquarters staff)
- **Civilian Employees**: ~200-300
- **Total Personnel**: ~1,000-1,300
- **Fleet Assets**: Approximately 100+ ships, 400+ aircraft, and 60,000 personnel when fully mobilized

## Headquarters
- **Location**: Naval Base Point Loma, San Diego, California
- **Facility**: Third Fleet Maritime Operations Center

## Command Structure
- Reports to U.S. Pacific Fleet
- Exercises operational control of assigned naval forces
- Provides forces to U.S. Indo-Pacific Command when required
- Participates in the "Third Fleet Forward" initiative, allowing operational command outside traditional boundaries

## Major Units and Assets
- Multiple carrier strike groups (when assigned)
- Expeditionary strike groups
- Surface action groups
- Submarines and submarine groups
- Naval air forces
- Mine warfare forces
- Fleet auxiliary ships

## History
The Third Fleet was originally formed during World War II on March 15, 1943, under the command of Admiral William F. Halsey Jr. The fleet played a critical role in major Pacific campaigns, including the Battle of Leyte Gulf and operations against Japan. After World War II, Third Fleet was deactivated but was recommissioned in February 1973 as part of a reorganization of the U.S. Pacific Fleet. In 2015, the fleet's operational role expanded under the "Third Fleet Forward" concept, which allows it to maintain command and control of assigned units throughout the Pacific theater, even when operating in the Seventh Fleet's traditional area of operations.

## Current Mission
Third Fleet's mission is to provide maritime security operations, disaster relief, and theater security cooperation activities with regional partners. It leads naval forces in the Eastern Pacific from the West Coast of North America to the International Date Line, supporting U.S. Indo-Pacific Command.
