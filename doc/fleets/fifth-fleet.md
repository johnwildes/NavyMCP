# U.S. 5th Fleet

## Overview
The United States Fifth Fleet is a numbered fleet of the United States Navy responsible for naval operations in the Persian Gulf, Red Sea, Arabian Sea, and parts of the Indian Ocean. It is one of the most strategically significant naval forces, operating in one of the world's most volatile regions to ensure maritime security and stability.

## Area of Operations
- **Primary Region**: Middle East maritime environment
- **Coverage Area**: Persian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Gulf of Aden, Red Sea, Arabian Sea
- **Strategic Focus**: Strait of Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb Strait, and Suez Canal (critical maritime chokepoints)
- **Countries**: Covers water adjacent to 20 countries including Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Qatar, UAE, and Yemen

## Personnel
- **Active Duty**: ~1,000-1,500 personnel (headquarters)
- **Civilian Employees**: ~200-300
- **Total Personnel**: ~1,200-1,800
- **Fleet Assets**: Approximately 20-30 ships, 100+ aircraft, and 20,000-25,000 sailors in the region at any given time

## Headquarters
- **Location**: Naval Support Activity Bahrain, Manama, Bahrain
- **Facility**: Fifth Fleet Maritime Operations Center

## Command Structure
- Dual-hatted command: Commander is also Commander, U.S. Naval Forces Central Command (NAVCENT)
- Reports to U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM)
- Operates combined maritime task forces with allied nations
- Maintains close coordination with Gulf Cooperation Council nations

## Major Units and Assets
- At least one Carrier Strike Group
- Amphibious Ready Group with Marine Expeditionary Unit
- Guided missile cruisers and destroyers
- Mine countermeasure ships
- Coastal patrol ships
- Expeditionary sea bases
- Task Force 50 (Strike Force)
- Task Force 51/5 (Expeditionary Operations)
- Task Force 52 (Mine Warfare)
- Task Force 53 (Logistics)
- Task Force 55 (Surface Forces)
- Task Force 56 (Expeditionary Combat Forces)
- Task Force 57 (Maritime Patrol Aircraft)
- Task Force 58 (Maritime Operations Center)
- Task Force 59 (Unmanned Systems and Artificial Intelligence Integration)

## History
The original Fifth Fleet was established during World War II in 1944 and disestablished after the war in 1947. It was reestablished in 1995 in response to increased tensions in the Middle East following the Gulf War, formalizing the naval component of U.S. Central Command that had been operating in the region since 1983. The Fifth Fleet's area of responsibility has been at the center of numerous conflicts and crises over the past three decades, including Operations Desert Shield/Desert Storm, maritime security operations following the September 11 attacks, counter-piracy operations off the Horn of Africa, and tensions with Iran over freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz. The Fleet has played a crucial role in projecting American power in the region and ensuring the free flow of commerce through vital maritime chokepoints.

## Current Mission
Fifth Fleet conducts maritime security operations, theater security cooperation efforts, and strengthens partner nations' maritime capabilities in order to ensure the free flow of commerce and freedom of navigation in the U.S. Central Command area of responsibility. It focuses particularly on countering maritime threats, deterring aggressive actions by regional adversaries (primarily Iran), combating piracy, and conducting humanitarian assistance/disaster relief operations when needed.
