# E-2D Advanced Hawkeye

**Type:** Carrier-based airborne early warning and control (AEW&C) aircraft
**Manufacturer:** Northrop Grumman
**First Flight:** August 3, 2007
**Introduction to Service:** October 2014
**Status:** Active

## Specifications
- **Crew:** 5 (2 pilots, 3 naval flight officers as combat information center officers)
- **Length:** 57 ft 7 in (17.56 m)
- **Wingspan:** 80 ft 7 in (24.56 m)
- **Height:** 18 ft 3 in (5.56 m)
- **Wing Area:** 700 sq ft (65 sq m)
- **Empty Weight:** 40,200 lb (18,234 kg)
- **Max Takeoff Weight:** 57,500 lb (26,083 kg)
- **Powerplant:** 2 × Rolls-Royce T56-A-427A turboprop engines, 5,100 hp (3,800 kW) each

## Performance
- **Maximum Speed:** 350 knots (400 mph, 650 km/h)
- **Cruise Speed:** 256 knots (295 mph, 474 km/h)
- **Range:** 1,462 nmi (1,682 mi, 2,708 km)
- **Endurance:** 6+ hours (with aerial refueling capability extending this significantly)
- **Service Ceiling:** 41,000 ft (12,500 m)

## Systems
- **AN/APY-9 Radar:** Ultra-high-frequency (UHF) radar with 360-degree coverage capable of detecting airborne threats at ranges greater than 350 miles
- **Cooperative Engagement Capability (CEC):** Advanced system allowing data sharing with other naval platforms
- **Advanced Computer and Display Systems:** Multiple workstations with modern human-computer interface
- **Enhanced Communications Suite:** Including satellite communications and Link-16
- **IFF System:** Advanced identification friend or foe systems
- **Electronic Support Measures (ESM):** For passive detection of electronic emissions
- **In-Flight Refueling:** Capability to extend mission duration

## Fleet Information
- **Inventory:** Approximately 50 in active service (as of 2025)
- **Unit Cost:** $176 million (2023)

## Deployment
The E-2D Advanced Hawkeye is deployed aboard aircraft carriers as part of Carrier Air Wings (CVWs).

### Carrier Air Wings Assignment:
Each Carrier Air Wing includes one E-2D squadron (typically 4-5 aircraft).

## Mission
The E-2D Advanced Hawkeye is a significant upgrade from previous E-2 Hawkeye variants, serving as the "eyes" of the fleet. Primary missions include:

- Airborne Early Warning (AEW)
- Battle Management Command and Control (BMC2)
- Air Traffic Control
- Surface Surveillance Coordination
- Strike Warfare Support
- Communications Relay
- Search and Rescue Coordination
- Drug Interdiction Operations Support

## Capabilities
- **Advanced Radar:** Can track more than 2,000 targets simultaneously
- **Cruise Missile Defense:** Enhanced capability to detect cruise missiles over land and water
- **Network-Centric Warfare:** Integrates with naval task forces to extend battlespace awareness
- **Theater Air and Missile Defense (TAMD):** Provides early detection of missile threats
- **Improved Over-the-Horizon Targeting:** Enhances fleet strike capabilities

## History
The E-2D Advanced Hawkeye is the latest variant of the E-2 Hawkeye aircraft that has been in service since the 1960s. It features significant technological improvements, including an entirely new radar system, upgraded mission computer, and tactical workstations. The E-2D achieved Initial Operational Capability (IOC) in 2014, and Full Operational Capability in 2017. The U.S. Navy plans to procure 75 E-2D aircraft by 2027. Japan, France, and other nations have also procured the E-2D to enhance their maritime security capabilities.
