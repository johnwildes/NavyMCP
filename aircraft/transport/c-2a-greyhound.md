# C-2A Greyhound

**Type:** Carrier onboard delivery (COD) aircraft
**Manufacturer:** Grumman (now Northrop Grumman)
**First Flight:** 1964
**Introduction to Service:** 1966
**Status:** Active, being phased out

## Variants
- **C-2A Reprocured (C-2A(R))**: Updated variant with improved engines and avionics

## Specifications
- **Crew:** 4 (2 pilots, 2 loadmasters)
- **Passenger Capacity:** 26 passengers or 10,000 lb (4,536 kg) of cargo
- **Length:** 56 ft 8 in (17.3 m)
- **Wingspan:** 80 ft 7 in (24.6 m)
- **Height:** 15 ft 11 in (4.84 m)
- **Wing Area:** 700 sq ft (65 sq m)
- **Empty Weight:** 33,746 lb (15,307 kg)
- **Max Takeoff Weight:** 57,500 lb (26,082 kg)
- **Powerplant:** 2 × Allison T56-A-425 turboprop engines, 4,600 shp (3,430 kW) each

## Performance
- **Maximum Speed:** 343 knots (395 mph, 635 km/h)
- **Cruise Speed:** 288 knots (331 mph, 533 km/h)
- **Range:** 1,300 nmi (1,496 mi, 2,408 km)
- **Ferry Range:** 1,700 nmi (1,956 mi, 3,148 km)
- **Service Ceiling:** 30,000 ft (9,144 m)
- **Rate of Climb:** 1,950 ft/min (9.9 m/s)

## Fleet Information
- **Inventory:** Approximately 27 in active service (as of 2025)
- **Unit Cost:** $38.96 million (reproduction cost)

## Deployment
The C-2A Greyhound is deployed aboard aircraft carriers to transport personnel, mail, supplies, and high-priority cargo between carriers and shore bases.

### Primary Deployment Locations:
- **Naval Air Station North Island, California**
  - VRC-30 "Providers"
- **Naval Air Station Norfolk, Virginia**
  - VRC-40 "Rawhides"

## Mission
The C-2A Greyhound primarily serves as a logistics aircraft for the U.S. Navy's carrier fleet. Its missions include:

- Carrier onboard delivery (COD) of personnel and cargo
- Transport of high-priority parts and equipment
- Medical evacuation (MEDEVAC) from carriers
- Mail delivery to deployed carrier strike groups
- Transport of distinguished visitors

## Features
- **Rear Loading Ramp:** For efficient loading/unloading of cargo
- **Folding Wings:** For carrier deck storage
- **Reinforced Landing Gear:** For carrier operations
- **Cargo Handling System:** Rollers and tie-downs for securing cargo
- **Navigation Systems:** Advanced avionics for over-water navigation
- **Defensive Systems:** Basic countermeasures for operational security

## History
The C-2 Greyhound was developed in the early 1960s from the E-2 Hawkeye airframe as a dedicated carrier onboard delivery (COD) aircraft. The original C-2A aircraft served from 1966 until the late 1980s when they were replaced by new C-2A(R) Reprocured aircraft with improved capabilities. For over five decades, the Greyhound has provided critical logistics support to carrier strike groups, enabling sustained operations at sea by delivering essential personnel, parts, and supplies. The C-2A fleet has undergone several service life extension programs to maintain operational capability. Beginning in 2021, the Navy started replacing the C-2A with the CMV-22B Osprey for the COD mission, with full transition expected to be complete by 2027.
