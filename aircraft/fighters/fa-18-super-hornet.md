# F/A-18E/F Super Hornet

**Type:** Carrier-based multirole fighter aircraft
**Manufacturer:** Boeing
**First Flight:** 1995
**Introduction to Service:** 1999
**Status:** Active

## Variants
- **F/A-18E**: Single-seat variant
- **F/A-18F**: Two-seat variant

## Specifications
- **Crew:** 1 (E variant) or 2 (F variant)
- **Length:** 60 ft 1 in (18.31 m)
- **Wingspan:** 44 ft 8.5 in (13.62 m)
- **Height:** 16 ft (4.88 m)
- **Wing Area:** 500 sq ft (46.5 sq m)
- **Empty Weight:** 30,564 lb (13,864 kg)
- **Max Takeoff Weight:** 66,000 lb (29,937 kg)
- **Powerplant:** 2 × General Electric F414-GE-400 turbofans, 22,000 lbf (98 kN) thrust each with afterburner

## Performance
- **Maximum Speed:** Mach 1.8 (1,190 mph, 1,915 km/h)
- **Combat Radius:** 449 mi (722 km) for interdiction mission
- **Service Ceiling:** 50,000+ ft (15,000+ m)
- **Rate of Climb:** 44,882 ft/min (228 m/s)

## Armament
- **Guns:** 1 × 20 mm (0.787 in) M61A2 Vulcan rotary cannon with 400 rounds
- **Hardpoints:** 11 total: 2 wingtips, 6 under-wing, and 3 under-fuselage with capacity for 17,750 lb (8,050 kg) of ordnance
- **Missiles:**
  - Air-to-air: AIM-9 Sidewinder, AIM-7 Sparrow, AIM-120 AMRAAM
  - Air-to-surface: AGM-65 Maverick, AGM-84 Harpoon, AGM-88 HARM, AGM-154 JSOW, AGM-158 JASSM
- **Bombs:** Various including JDAM precision-guided munitions, Paveway laser-guided bombs, Mk 80 series unguided bombs, nuclear weapons (B61 nuclear bomb)

## Fleet Information
- **Inventory:** Approximately 550 in active service (as of 2025)
- **Unit Cost:** $70.5 million (2023)

## Deployment
The F/A-18E/F Super Hornets are primarily deployed aboard aircraft carriers as part of Carrier Air Wings (CVWs).

### Primary Deployment Locations:
- **Naval Air Station Oceana, Virginia**
  - Strike Fighter Wing Atlantic
  - Multiple squadrons including VFA-103, VFA-105, VFA-106, VFA-136, VFA-143, VFA-211, VFA-31, VFA-87
- **Naval Air Station Lemoore, California**
  - Strike Fighter Wing Pacific
  - Multiple squadrons including VFA-2, VFA-14, VFA-22, VFA-25, VFA-41, VFA-86, VFA-94, VFA-97, VFA-113, VFA-115, VFA-122, VFA-137, VFA-151, VFA-154, VFA-192

### Carrier Air Wings Assignment:
Super Hornets are assigned to all ten active US Navy Carrier Air Wings, with typically 4-5 squadrons of F/A-18E/F variants per air wing.

## Mission
The F/A-18E/F Super Hornet is a twin-engine, carrier-capable multirole fighter aircraft. It serves as both a fighter (air-to-air) and attack (air-to-surface) aircraft. Primary missions include:

- Fleet air defense
- Fighter escort
- Close air support
- Air interdiction
- Enemy air defense suppression
- Reconnaissance
- Forward air control
- Day/night strike with precision-guided weapons

## History
The Super Hornet is a major redesign of the F/A-18C/D Hornet, with 25% larger airframe, increased range and payload capacity, and more powerful engines. It entered service with the United States Navy in 1999 as a replacement for the F-14 Tomcat. The Super Hornet has served in Operation Enduring Freedom, Operation Iraqi Freedom, and Operation Inherent Resolve, providing close air support and precision strike capabilities.
