# F-35C Lightning II

**Type:** Carrier-based multirole stealth fighter aircraft
**Manufacturer:** Lockheed Martin
**First Flight:** 2010
**Introduction to Service:** 2019
**Status:** Active

## Variants
- **F-35C**: Carrier-based variant for the U.S. Navy

## Specifications
- **Crew:** 1
- **Length:** 51 ft 5 in (15.67 m)
- **Wingspan:** 43 ft (13 m)
- **Height:** 14 ft 7 in (4.44 m)
- **Wing Area:** 668 sq ft (62.1 sq m)
- **Empty Weight:** 34,800 lb (15,785 kg)
- **Max Takeoff Weight:** 70,000 lb (31,751 kg)
- **Powerplant:** 1 × Pratt & Whitney F135-PW-400 afterburning turbofan, 40,000 lbf (178 kN) thrust with afterburner

## Performance
- **Maximum Speed:** Mach 1.6 (1,042 mph, 1,676 km/h)
- **Combat Radius:** >670 nmi (1,241 km)
- **Service Ceiling:** 50,000+ ft (15,000+ m)
- **Rate of Climb:** 45,000+ ft/min (229+ m/s)

## Armament
- **Guns:** 1 × 25 mm GAU-22/A 4-barrel rotary cannon with 220 rounds
- **Hardpoints:** 6 internal, 4 external, with a capacity for 18,000 lb (8,160 kg) of ordnance
- **Missiles:**
  - Air-to-air: AIM-9X Sidewinder, AIM-120 AMRAAM
  - Air-to-surface: AGM-154 JSOW, AGM-158 JASSM
- **Bombs:** Various including GBU-31/32/38 JDAM precision-guided bombs, GBU-12/16/10 Paveway II/III laser-guided bombs, Mk 84/83/82 general-purpose bombs

## Fleet Information
- **Inventory:** Approximately 125 in active service (as of 2025)
- **Unit Cost:** $94.4 million (2023)

## Deployment
The F-35C Lightning II is deployed aboard aircraft carriers as part of Carrier Air Wings (CVWs), gradually replacing legacy F/A-18C Hornets.

### Primary Deployment Locations:
- **Naval Air Station Lemoore, California**
  - VFA-97 "Warhawks"
  - VFA-125 "Rough Raiders" (Fleet Replacement Squadron)
  - VFA-147 "Argonauts"
  - VFA-94 "Mighty Shrikes"
  - VFA-146 "Blue Diamonds"
- **Naval Air Station Oceana, Virginia**
  - VFA-143 "Pukin Dogs"
  - VFA-106 "Gladiators" (Fleet Replacement Squadron)

### Carrier Air Wings Assignment:
F-35C squadrons are being integrated into Carrier Air Wings, with plans to have at least one F-35C squadron in each air wing by 2027.

## Mission
The F-35C Lightning II is the carrier variant of the F-35 Joint Strike Fighter, designed to operate from aircraft carriers. It features larger wings and reinforced landing gear for the carrier environment. Primary missions include:

- Air superiority
- Deep strike missions
- Suppression of enemy air defenses
- Intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR)
- Electronic warfare
- Maritime strike

## Features
- **Stealth Technology:** Very low observable radar cross-section
- **Advanced Sensor Fusion:** Combines data from onboard and offboard sensors into a single tactical picture
- **Distributed Aperture System (DAS):** Provides 360-degree situational awareness
- **Electro-Optical Targeting System (EOTS):** Advanced integrated targeting system
- **Helmet-Mounted Display System:** Allows pilots to see through the aircraft structure
- **Interoperability:** Enhanced data sharing with other platforms

## History
The F-35C is the U.S. Navy's first stealth fighter aircraft designed specifically for carrier operations. After extensive testing, the F-35C reached Initial Operational Capability in February 2019. In 2021, the F-35C completed its first operational deployment aboard USS Carl Vinson as part of Carrier Air Wing 2. The F-35C represents a significant technological advancement for carrier aviation, bringing 5th-generation fighter capabilities to the Navy's Carrier Air Wings.
