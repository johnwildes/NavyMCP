# U.S. 6th Fleet

## Overview
The United States Sixth Fleet is a numbered fleet of the United States Navy operating as part of U.S. Naval Forces Europe. It is the operational arm of U.S. naval forces in Europe and Africa, conducting the full range of maritime operations to support U.S. national interests and security in the European and African theatres.

## Area of Operations
- **Primary Region**: Mediterranean Sea, parts of the Atlantic Ocean
- **Coverage Area**: Mediterranean Sea, Black Sea, parts of the Atlantic Ocean from the Strait of Gibraltar to the coast of West Africa
- **Strategic Focus**: European maritime approaches, Black Sea region, North Africa, and parts of West Africa
- **Countries**: Operations adjacent to European and African coastal nations

## Personnel
- **Active Duty**: ~800-1,000 personnel (headquarters)
- **Civilian Employees**: ~150-200
- **Total Personnel**: ~950-1,200
- **Fleet Assets**: Varies by deployment, typically includes 5-10 ships, submarines, and aircraft squadrons, with 4,000-8,000 sailors and Marines

## Headquarters
- **Location**: Naval Support Activity Naples, Italy
- **Facility**: Capodichino Complex, Naples

## Command Structure
- Dual-hatted command: Commander is also Deputy Commander, U.S. Naval Forces Europe-Africa
- Reports to Commander, U.S. Naval Forces Europe-Africa
- Works closely with NATO Allied Maritime Command
- Operates as the naval component of U.S. European Command (EUCOM) and U.S. Africa Command (AFRICOM)

## Major Units and Assets
- Flagship: USS Mount Whitney (LCC-20), command ship
- Typically one Destroyer Squadron
- Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyers based in Rota, Spain
- Submarines (when assigned)
- Maritime patrol and reconnaissance aircraft
- Carrier Strike Group 8 (when deployed to the region)
- Task Force 61/2 (Amphibious and Marine)
- Task Force 62 (Landing Force)
- Task Force 63 (Logistics)
- Task Force 64 (Special Operations)
- Task Force 65 (Surface Combatants)
- Task Force 66 (Submarine Warfare)
- Task Force 67 (Maritime Patrol Aircraft)
- Task Force 68 (Maritime Expeditionary Forces)
- Task Force 69 (Submarine Warfare)

## History
The Sixth Fleet was established in 1950, evolving from U.S. naval forces that had been present in the Mediterranean since the end of World War II. Throughout the Cold War, the Sixth Fleet served as a critical counterbalance to Soviet naval forces in the Mediterranean, conducting regular "freedom of navigation" operations and serving as a powerful symbol of U.S. commitment to European allies. The fleet played essential roles during various Cold War crises, including the 1956 Suez Crisis, the 1967 Arab-Israeli War, and the 1973 Yom Kippur War. Following the Cold War, the Sixth Fleet's mission expanded to include crisis response, humanitarian operations, and counterterrorism. In more recent years, it has supported operations in Libya (2011), provided ballistic missile defense to European allies, responded to Russian naval expansion in the Black Sea, and conducted security cooperation with African partners as part of a broader maritime security strategy.

## Current Mission
The Sixth Fleet conducts operational missions that include promoting regional stability, deterring aggression, responding to crises, supporting NATO operations and partnerships, providing ballistic missile defense for Europe, and conducting security cooperation activities with partner nations across Europe and Africa. Its presence demonstrates U.S. commitment to allies and partners while providing rapidly deployable naval forces for contingency operations.
