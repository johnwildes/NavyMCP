# U.S. 2nd Fleet

## Overview
The United States Second Fleet is a numbered fleet in the United States Navy responsible for the North Atlantic Ocean. The Second Fleet was reestablished on August 24, 2018, in response to increasing tensions with Russia, particularly in the North Atlantic region. It operates as an operational level command with a focus on forward operations and the development of combat-ready naval forces.

## Area of Operations
- **Primary Region**: North Atlantic Ocean
- **Coverage Area**: Western Atlantic and northern Atlantic approaches to the United States
- **Strategic Focus**: Defense of the sea lanes between the United States and Europe, Arctic naval operations

## Personnel
- **Active Duty**: ~300-500 personnel
- **Civilian Employees**: ~100
- **Total Personnel**: ~400-600

## Headquarters
- **Location**: Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia
- **Facility**: Admirals Row at Naval Support Activity Hampton Roads

## Command Structure
- Reports directly to U.S. Fleet Forces Command
- Coordinates with NATO Allied Joint Force Command Norfolk
- Maintains operational control of assigned naval forces

## Major Units and Assets
- Aircraft carriers and carrier strike groups (when assigned)
- Surface combatants
- Submarines
- Expeditionary forces
- Maritime patrol aircraft
- Support vessels

## History
Originally established in 1950 as part of the United States Atlantic Fleet, the Second Fleet played a crucial role throughout the Cold War in projecting American naval power across the North Atlantic and maintaining sea lanes between North America and Europe. The fleet was disestablished in 2011 as part of cost-saving measures and consolidation of command structures. However, with growing Russian naval activity and increased strategic competition in the North Atlantic, the U.S. Navy reestablished the Second Fleet in 2018. Its modern mission emphasizes high-end naval warfare and the development of concepts for operating in contested environments, particularly in the increasingly strategic Arctic region.

## Current Mission
The Second Fleet's mission is to develop and dynamically employ maritime forces ready to fight across multiple domains in the Atlantic and Arctic in order to ensure access, deter aggression, and defend U.S., allied, and partner interests.
