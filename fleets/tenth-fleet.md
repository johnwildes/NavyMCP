# U.S. 10th Fleet

## Overview
The United States Tenth Fleet, also known as Fleet Cyber Command, is the functional fleet responsible for the U.S. Navy's cyber warfare, electronic warfare, information operations, cryptology, and signals intelligence capabilities. Unlike traditional numbered fleets that operate ships in geographic areas, the Tenth Fleet is responsible for the Navy's cyberspace operations worldwide.

## Area of Operations
- **Primary Region**: Cyberspace (global)
- **Coverage Area**: Global cyber domain
- **Strategic Focus**: Defense of Navy networks, offensive cyber operations, signals intelligence
- **Domains**: Operations in cyberspace supporting all geographic combatant commands

## Personnel
- **Active Duty**: ~6,000 personnel across all components
- **Civilian Employees**: ~1,000
- **Total Personnel**: ~7,000 (including contractors)
- **Note**: Personnel distributed across multiple global facilities

## Headquarters
- **Location**: Fort Meade, Maryland
- **Facility**: Fleet Cyber Command/U.S. Tenth Fleet Headquarters (co-located with NSA and U.S. Cyber Command)

## Command Structure
- Dual-hatted command: Commander is also Commander, U.S. Fleet Cyber Command
- Reports to both U.S. Cyber Command and the Chief of Naval Operations
- Oversees Navy Information Operations Commands (NIOCs) globally
- Coordinates with Naval Computer and Telecommunications Area Master Stations

## Major Units and Assets
- Navy Information Operations Command (NIOC) Maryland
- NIOC Georgia
- NIOC Texas
- NIOC Colorado
- NIOC Hawaii
- Naval Computer and Telecommunications Station (NCTS) units worldwide
- Cyber Mission Forces including:
  - Cyber Protection Teams
  - Combat Mission Teams
  - National Mission Teams
  - Cyber Support Teams
- Naval Network Warfare Command elements

## History
The original U.S. Tenth Fleet was established in May 1943 during World War II as an anti-submarine warfare unit tasked with combatting the German U-boat threat in the Battle of the Atlantic. This original Tenth Fleet was decommissioned after the war in 1945. In a historic move recognizing the importance of the cyber domain to modern warfare, the Navy reestablished the Tenth Fleet in January 2010 as the operational arm of Fleet Cyber Command. The revival of this historic fleet designation for cyber operations symbolized the Navy's recognition that cyberspace had become as critical a warfighting domain as the sea. Since its reestablishment, the Tenth Fleet has continually evolved its capabilities to address growing cyber threats to naval operations and critical infrastructure, while developing sophisticated offensive capabilities to support national security objectives.

## Current Mission
The Tenth Fleet's mission is to serve as the Navy component command to U.S. Cyber Command, the Navy service cryptologic component to the National Security Agency, and the Navy's operational authority for information operations, electronic warfare, and signals intelligence. It is responsible for directing and coordinating cyber warfare operations, defending the Navy's portion of the Department of Defense Information Network, achieving military objectives in and through cyberspace, and enabling naval and joint operations through integration of cyber capabilities.
