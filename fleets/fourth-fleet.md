# U.S. 4th Fleet

## Overview
The United States Fourth Fleet is a numbered fleet in the United States Navy that operates in the Southern Command area of responsibility, covering Central and South America and the Caribbean. The Fourth Fleet was reestablished in 2008 after being deactivated since 1950, reflecting the renewed focus on maritime security and cooperation in the region.

## Area of Operations
- **Primary Region**: Caribbean Sea, Central and South America
- **Coverage Area**: Waters surrounding Central and South America, including the Caribbean Sea, Atlantic and Pacific Oceans
- **Strategic Focus**: Counter-narcotics operations, humanitarian assistance/disaster relief, maritime security cooperation

## Personnel
- **Active Duty**: ~160-200 personnel
- **Civilian Employees**: ~40-60
- **Total Personnel**: ~200-260
- **Note**: The Fourth Fleet primarily operates as a command and control organization without permanently assigned ships or aircraft

## Headquarters
- **Location**: Naval Station Mayport, Jacksonville, Florida
- **Facility**: Shares headquarters with U.S. Naval Forces Southern Command (USNAVSO)

## Command Structure
- Dual-hatted command: Commander is also Commander, U.S. Naval Forces Southern Command
- Reports to U.S. Southern Command (SOUTHCOM)
- Coordinates with partner nations throughout Latin America and the Caribbean
- Exercises operational control of assigned naval forces during deployments and exercises

## Major Units and Assets
- No permanently assigned forces
- Employs forces temporarily assigned from:
  - Carrier Strike Groups (during deployments)
  - Amphibious Ready Groups
  - Hospital ships (USNS Comfort for humanitarian missions)
  - Littoral Combat Ships
  - Maritime patrol aircraft
  - Coast Guard vessels (through joint operations)

## History
The original Fourth Fleet was created in 1943 during World War II to protect against German U-boats in the South Atlantic. After the war, it was disbanded in 1950. For nearly 60 years, naval operations in Latin America and the Caribbean were managed by U.S. Atlantic Fleet. In 2008, the Fourth Fleet was reestablished to focus on regional maritime security cooperation and counter transnational threats, including narcotics trafficking and terrorism. The reestablishment also reflected the growing importance of the region to U.S. national security and economic interests. Despite some initial concerns from countries in the region about increased U.S. military presence, the Fourth Fleet has focused primarily on building partnerships and cooperative security activities.

## Current Mission
The Fourth Fleet conducts maritime security operations through theater security cooperation activities, military-to-military interaction, and bilateral and multinational training. Key focuses include counter-illicit trafficking operations, humanitarian assistance/disaster relief, and supporting partner nations' maritime security capabilities. The fleet also operates to defend the Panama Canal and ensure freedom of navigation in the region.
