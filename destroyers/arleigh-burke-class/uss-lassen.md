# USS Lassen (DDG-82)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Lieutenant Clyde Everett Lassen
**Commissioned:** April 21, 2001
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Mayport, Florida

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Lassen is the thirty-second ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Lieutenant Clyde Everett Lassen, a naval helicopter pilot who was awarded the Medal of Honor for his actions during the Vietnam War, where he rescued two downed aviators while under intense enemy fire.

The ship was commissioned on April 21, 2001, and was formerly forward-deployed to Yokosuka, Japan as part of the U.S. 7th Fleet before being reassigned to Naval Station Mayport. USS Lassen gained international attention in 2015 when she conducted a freedom of navigation operation within 12 nautical miles of the Chinese-built artificial islands in the South China Sea.
