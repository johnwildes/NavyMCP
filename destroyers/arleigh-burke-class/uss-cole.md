# USS Cole (DDG-67)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight I)
**Namesake:** Sergeant Darrell S. Cole
**Commissioned:** June 8, 1996
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 8,230 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 505 ft (154 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/54 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 1 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 1 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopter

## History
USS Cole is the seventeenth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Sergeant Darrell S. Cole, a Marine who was posthumously awarded the Medal of Honor for his actions during the Battle of Iwo Jima in World War II. 

The ship gained international attention on October 12, 2000, when she was attacked by suicide bombers while harbored in the Yemeni port of Aden. The terrorist attack killed 17 sailors and injured 39 others. Following extensive repairs, USS Cole returned to service and continues to serve as a symbol of naval resilience and determination.
