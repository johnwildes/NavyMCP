# USS Michael Murphy (DDG-112)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Lieutenant Michael P. Murphy
**Commissioned:** October 6, 2012
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Pearl Harbor, Hawaii

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Michael Murphy is the sixty-second ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers and the last of the original Flight IIA variant. She is named for Lieutenant Michael P. Murphy, a Navy SEAL who was posthumously awarded the Medal of Honor for his actions during Operation Red Wings in Afghanistan in 2005. Lieutenant Murphy sacrificed his life by exposing himself to enemy fire in order to call for help for his team.

The ship was commissioned on October 6, 2012, and is homeported at Naval Station Pearl Harbor, Hawaii. USS Michael Murphy regularly deploys throughout the Indo-Pacific region and participates in multinational exercises with allied navies. The ship is equipped with advanced Aegis combat systems and ballistic missile defense capabilities, continuing the legacy of its namesake's dedication to service and sacrifice.
