# USS Wayne E. Meyer (DDG-108)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Rear Admiral Wayne E. Meyer
**Commissioned:** October 10, 2009
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Wayne E. Meyer is the fifty-eighth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Rear Admiral Wayne E. Meyer, who is regarded as the "Father of Aegis" for his leading role in the development of the Aegis Combat System, the advanced automated command-and-decision system that forms the backbone of the Navy's surface fleet today.

The ship was commissioned on October 10, 2009, and has participated in numerous operations throughout the Pacific. USS Wayne E. Meyer regularly conducts freedom of navigation operations in contested waters and has participated in multinational exercises with allied navies. The ship is equipped with advanced Aegis combat systems and ballistic missile defense capabilities, carrying on the legacy of its namesake.
