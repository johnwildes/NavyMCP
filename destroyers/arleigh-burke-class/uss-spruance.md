# USS Spruance (DDG-111)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Admiral Raymond A. Spruance
**Commissioned:** October 1, 2011
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Spruance is the sixty-first ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Admiral Raymond A. Spruance, who commanded U.S. naval forces at the pivotal Battle of Midway in World War II and later commanded the Fifth Fleet during the battles of Iwo Jima and Okinawa.

The ship was commissioned on October 1, 2011, and has participated in numerous operations throughout the Pacific and Indian Ocean regions. USS Spruance regularly conducts freedom of navigation operations and has been deployed as part of carrier strike groups for regional security operations. The ship is equipped with advanced Aegis combat systems and ballistic missile defense capabilities.
