# USS Bulkeley (DDG-84)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Vice Admiral John Duncan Bulkeley
**Commissioned:** December 8, 2001
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Bulkeley is the thirty-fourth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Vice Admiral John Duncan Bulkeley, a Medal of Honor recipient who commanded PT boats in the Philippines during World War II. Most notably, Bulkeley evacuated General Douglas MacArthur, his family, and his staff from Corregidor to Mindanao in 1942.

The ship was commissioned on December 8, 2001, and has participated in numerous operations including deployments to the Mediterranean Sea and the Persian Gulf in support of maritime security operations and the Global War on Terror.
