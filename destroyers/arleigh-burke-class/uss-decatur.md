# USS Decatur (DDG-73)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight I)
**Namesake:** Stephen Decatur
**Commissioned:** August 29, 1998
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 8,230 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 505 ft (154 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/54 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 1 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 1 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopter

## History
USS Decatur is the twenty-third ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Stephen Decatur Jr., a naval officer notable for his heroism in the Barbary Wars and the War of 1812. He is famous for his toast: "Our country, right or wrong!"

The ship was commissioned on August 29, 1998, and has participated in numerous operations in the Western Pacific. In 2018, USS Decatur made headlines when it was involved in a close encounter with a Chinese warship in the South China Sea during a freedom of navigation operation.
