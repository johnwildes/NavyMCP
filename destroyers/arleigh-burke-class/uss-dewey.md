# USS Dewey (DDG-105)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Admiral of the Navy George Dewey
**Commissioned:** March 6, 2010
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Dewey is the fifty-fifth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Admiral of the Navy George Dewey, who is best known for his victory at the Battle of Manila Bay during the Spanish-American War in 1898. Dewey was the only person in U.S. history to attain the rank of Admiral of the Navy, the highest possible rank in the U.S. Navy.

The ship was commissioned on March 6, 2010, and has participated in numerous operations throughout the Pacific. USS Dewey has been involved in freedom of navigation operations in the South China Sea and has been equipped with advanced combat systems, including testing of the Laser Weapon System (LaWS) in 2012.
