# USS Mahan (DDG-72)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight I)
**Namesake:** Rear Admiral Alfred Thayer Mahan
**Commissioned:** February 14, 1998
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 8,230 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 505 ft (154 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/54 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 1 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 1 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopter

## History
USS Mahan is the twenty-second ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Rear Admiral Alfred Thayer Mahan, a naval strategist and historian who greatly influenced the development of naval warfare at the turn of the 20th century. His book "The Influence of Sea Power Upon History, 1660–1783" is considered one of the most influential works on naval strategy.

The ship was commissioned on February 14, 1998, and has participated in numerous operations including counter-piracy operations and deployments to the Mediterranean Sea and the Persian Gulf.
