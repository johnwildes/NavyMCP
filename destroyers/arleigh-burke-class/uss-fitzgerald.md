# USS Fitzgerald (DDG-62)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight I)
**Namesake:** Lieutenant William Charles Fitzgerald
**Commissioned:** October 14, 1995
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Base San Diego, California

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 8,230 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 505 ft (154 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/54 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 1 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 1 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopter

## History
USS Fitzgerald is the twelfth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Lieutenant William Charles Fitzgerald, who was posthumously awarded the Navy Cross for his actions during the Vietnam War. The ship was commissioned on October 14, 1995 and was previously forward-deployed to Yokosuka, Japan before being reassigned to San Diego following a collision in 2017.
