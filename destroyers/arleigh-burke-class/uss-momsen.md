# USS Momsen (DDG-92)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Vice Admiral Charles B. "Swede" Momsen
**Commissioned:** August 28, 2004
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Everett, Washington

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Momsen is the forty-second ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers. She is named for Vice Admiral Charles B. "Swede" Momsen, who is best known for his invention of the "Momsen lung," a submarine escape breathing device, and for leading the rescue of the crew of the sunken submarine USS Squalus in 1939.

The ship was commissioned on August 28, 2004, and has participated in numerous operations including counter-piracy missions and maritime security operations in the Western Pacific and Indian Ocean. USS Momsen is equipped with advanced Aegis combat systems and has participated in various multinational exercises with allied navies.
