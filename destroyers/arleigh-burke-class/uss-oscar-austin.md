# USS Oscar Austin (DDG-79)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** Private First Class Oscar P. Austin
**Commissioned:** August 19, 2000
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Oscar Austin is the twenty-ninth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers and the first Flight IIA variant. She is named for Private First Class Oscar P. Austin, a United States Marine who was posthumously awarded the Medal of Honor for his heroism in the Vietnam War when he sacrificed his life to save a fellow Marine.

The ship was commissioned on August 19, 2000, and marked a significant upgrade to the Arleigh Burke class with the Flight IIA design, which included a helicopter hangar to accommodate two SH-60 helicopters. USS Oscar Austin has participated in numerous operations including counter-piracy missions and deployments to the Mediterranean Sea and the Persian Gulf.
