# USS Roosevelt (DDG-80)

**Class:** Arleigh Burke-class destroyer (Flight IIA)
**Namesake:** President Franklin D. Roosevelt and First Lady Eleanor Roosevelt
**Commissioned:** October 14, 2000
**Status:** In active service
**Homeport:** Naval Station Mayport, Florida

## Specifications
- **Displacement:** 9,200 tons (full load)
- **Length:** 509 ft (155 m)
- **Beam:** 66 ft (20 m)
- **Draft:** 31 ft (9.4 m)
- **Propulsion:** 4 General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, 100,000 shp (75 MW)
- **Speed:** 30+ knots (56+ km/h; 35+ mph)
- **Complement:** 380 officers and enlisted

## Armament
- 1 × 5-inch (127 mm)/62 caliber Mk 45 gun
- 2 × 25 mm Mk 38 chain guns
- 4 × .50-cal (12.7 mm) machine guns
- 2 × 20 mm Phalanx CIWS
- 1 × 32 cell, 1 × 64 cell Mk 41 vertical launch systems
- 90 × RIM-156 SM-2, BGM-109 Tomahawk, RUM-139 VL-ASROC
- 8 × RGM-84 Harpoon missiles
- 2 × Mk 32 triple torpedo tubes
- 6 × Mk 46 or Mk 50 torpedoes

## Aircraft
- 2 × SH-60 LAMPS III helicopters

## History
USS Roosevelt is the thirtieth ship of the Arleigh Burke class of guided missile destroyers and the second Flight IIA variant. She is named for both President Franklin D. Roosevelt and First Lady Eleanor Roosevelt, the only ship in the class named for two people.

The ship was commissioned on October 14, 2000, and features the Flight IIA design improvements, including a helicopter hangar to accommodate two SH-60 helicopters. USS Roosevelt has participated in numerous operations including deployments to the Mediterranean Sea and the Persian Gulf in support of maritime security operations.
