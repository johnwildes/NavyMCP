# USS Ronald Reagan (CVN-76)

## Characteristics
- **Ship Name**: USS Ronald Reagan
- **Hull Number**: CVN-76
- **Commissioned**: July 12, 2003
- **Displacement**: Approximately 101,400 long tons (103,000 t) full load
- **Length**: 1,092 feet (332.8 m)
- **Beam**: 134 feet (40.8 m) waterline, 252 feet (76.8 m) extreme
- **Draft**: 37 feet (11.3 m)
- **Power Plant**: 2 A4W nuclear reactors, 4 steam turbines, 4 shafts, 260,000 shaft horsepower
- **Speed**: 30+ knots (56+ km/h)
- **Crew Size**: Approximately 5,000 (Ship's company: 3,200, Air wing: 1,800)
- **Aircraft**: 60+ aircraft including F/A-18 Super Hornets, EA-18G Growlers, E-2 Hawkeyes, and helicopters

## History
The USS Ronald Reagan (CVN-76) was commissioned on July 12, 2003, named after the 40th President of the United States. Since 2015, the Reagan has served as the flagship of Carrier Strike Group 5 and the Navy's only forward-deployed aircraft carrier, operating from Yokosuka, Japan. The carrier has been instrumental in maintaining security and stability in the Indo-Pacific region, conducting numerous exercises with allied nations and responding to regional tensions, particularly regarding North Korea and China. In 2011, the Reagan played a significant role in Operation Tomodachi, providing disaster relief following the devastating Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami in Japan. As the forward-deployed carrier in one of the world's most strategically important regions, the Ronald Reagan continues to embody American commitment to regional allies while serving as a powerful deterrent to potential adversaries in an increasingly contested maritime environment.
