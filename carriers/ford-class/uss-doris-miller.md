# USS Doris Miller (CVN-81)

## Characteristics
- **Ship Name**: USS Doris Miller
- **Hull Number**: CVN-81
- **Commissioned**: Expected 2032
- **Displacement**: Approximately 100,000 long tons (100,000 t) full load
- **Length**: 1,106 feet (337 m)
- **Beam**: 134 feet (41 m) waterline, 256 feet (78 m) extreme
- **Draft**: 39 feet (12 m)
- **Power Plant**: Two A1B nuclear reactors, four shafts
- **Speed**: 30+ knots (56+ km/h)
- **Crew Size**: 2,600 (ship only; approximately 4,400 with full air wing)
- **Aircraft**: 75+ aircraft including F-35C Lightning II, F/A-18E/F Super Hornets, EA-18G Growlers, E-2D Advanced Hawkeyes, and helicopters

## History
The USS Doris Miller (CVN-81) is the fourth ship of the Ford-class and represents a historic milestone as the first aircraft carrier named after an enlisted sailor and the first named after an African American. Doris Miller was a Mess Attendant Third Class who displayed extraordinary courage during the attack on Pearl Harbor, for which he was awarded the Navy Cross. Construction on the carrier officially began with the first cut of steel in 2023, benefiting from a two-carrier procurement contract alongside USS Enterprise (CVN-80) that is expected to save the Navy approximately $4 billion. When completed, the Doris Miller will incorporate all the technological advancements of the Ford-class, with additional improvements based on operational experience with earlier ships of the class. The naming of this vessel reflects the Navy's commitment to honoring the contributions of all sailors regardless of race or rank and ensures that Miller's legacy of bravery and service will be remembered for generations to come.
